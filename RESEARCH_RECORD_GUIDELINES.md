# Research record

Maintain the manuscript-facing record alongside substantive changes to models,
planners, controllers, experiments, or interpretations:

- Append the change, rationale, validation and output locations to
  `docs/research/WORK_LOG.md`.
- Update `docs/research/CLAIMS.md` when evidence supports, weakens or limits a
  manuscript claim. Distinguish implemented, tested and proposed mechanisms.
- Record exact settings, seeds, simulator/planner versions, strategy and source
  hashes for new experiments. Preserve historical output directories.
- Keep curated numerical evidence and manuscript figures under `docs/`; bulk
  generated outputs under ignored `mulder_results/` are not the sole record.
- Separate software verification, computational model experiments and empirical
  biological validation. Do not describe imposed geometry as independent
  evidence, or an unimplemented literature algorithm as our implementation.

Read `docs/research/README.md` for the record layout and current limitations.
