# Mulder objective: status and route forward

Assessment date: 2026-10-20. This is a proposed research roadmap, not a record
of completed biological validation or a promise of publication.

The original objective remains to assess whether an HTN-based construction
model can account for Mulder et al.'s moving-versus-rigid observations. A
simulation can test proposed explanations and generate predictions; independently
verifying the animal observations requires empirical data or replication.

## What exists

- Apparatus-inspired moving-support simulation, six feedback/trigger policies,
  geometric/behavioral measurements, stationary controls and sensitivity runs.
- v3: 2,280 computational trials; ideal local tracking completes 30/30 primary
  moving runs, with roughly 0.98% increased construction time. The supplied flat
  sequence matches HTN local execution in the tested ablation.
- Recovery v1 and integrated Mulder v4 examine failure recovery. v4 has 2,160
  main trials and a ten-trial scope control. Whole-suffix refresh explains the
  selected lagged filtered gain; retained trees add no physical outcome advantage
  over fresh IPyHOP in 720 pairs. These are supporting computational studies.
- Tests, traces, manifests, curated evidence and manuscript fragments exist.

## The missing scientific bridge

The original cohort data have not been obtained or fitted. Apparatus contact,
sensing, speed and construction assumptions are uncalibrated. Prescribed counts
and targets, together with area-preserving shear, impose much of the geometric
stability. Phase seeds are not biological individuals. A non-significant animal
comparison is not proof of zero difference and should not be reproduced by
trying to match p-values.

Primary source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00114-021-01725-1
The paper states data and statistical code are available from the corresponding
author on reasonable request. No contact is authorized or sent by this roadmap.

## Ordered milestones and completion criteria

1. **Define the comparison.** Build a claim-to-measurement table for completion,
   total time, travel, construction-phase allocation and geometry. Mark every
   output as predicted, calibrated or imposed. Select a small primary outcome
   set and practical agreement criteria before tuning.
2. **Secure reference evidence.** Inventory all supplementary material and seek
   original per-spider/per-build observations, measurement definitions and code.
   If unavailable, clearly label figure-derived estimates and restrict claims;
   do not invent individual data or recreate paired uncertainty from group bars.
3. **Fit rigid controls only.** Estimate identifiable speed/geometry/contact
   parameters from stationary observations with uncertainty. Document which
   outcomes are fitting targets. Freeze those choices before testing moving data.
4. **Test moving predictions.** Compare measured effect sizes and uncertainty,
   not just completion or p-values. Account for repeated builds from the same
   spider. Report mismatches and sensitivity to sensing/contact assumptions.
5. **Test the role of hierarchy.** Compare the same movement model under local
   rules, a flat sequence and HTN methods. Add only justified alternatives and
   dependencies; larger action counts alone are not richer decision-making.
   Disturbance/damage experiments beyond the paper are explicitly extensions.

We need not build a complete silk-mechanics solver before making progress.
Start with the simplest defensible model and add mechanics only when a specific
measurement or failure exposes its necessity. More simulation seeds cannot
repair an unvalidated physical assumption or create new animal observations.

## Publication assessment

There is a credible foundation for a computational modeling/benchmark manuscript,
including informative negative results and reproducibility infrastructure.
There is not yet evidence for a biological-verification paper or a new general
planning algorithm. IPyHOP is existing prior work. A publishable contribution
needs a focused question, a defensible model, appropriate baseline comparisons,
a clear account of novelty relative to related work, and conclusions limited to
the evidence. Acceptance cannot be inferred from test counts or simulation volume.

The strongest current candidate question is when local feedback is sufficient
for moving-support construction and when broader replanning becomes useful.
The intended biological route additionally requires the calibrated, held-out
comparison above and nontrivial predictions. Data access is an external dependency;
claim mapping, measurement alignment and assessment of imposed outputs can proceed
immediately without changing the planner again.
